## 💡 Delivery Tips for Spoken Interviews

1. **The "Present-Past-Differentiator-Future" Flow:**
   - **Present:** Who you are + core identity (Software Engineer, Full-Stack & Systems).
   - **Past / Proof:** Internship at Amnesea (35% speed improvement) + Shree Mewa (production product) + Remote Trackpad (deep systems chops).
   - **Differentiator:** CSI national prize + AI-assisted rapid delivery + real product empathy.
   - **Future:** Why you are eager to contribute to *this* team.

2. **Avoid Passive Fresher Clichés:**
   - Instead of: *"I am a hardworking student eager to learn and ready to work in any technology..."*  
   - Say: *"I specialize in full-stack web and networked systems, with a track record of shipping production code and optimizing performance."*

3. **Control the Transition:**
   - Conclude your spoken intro with: *"I'd be glad to dive deeper into any of these projects or discuss how my background aligns with what you're building."* This gives the interviewer an easy, natural opening to ask about your strongest projects.
