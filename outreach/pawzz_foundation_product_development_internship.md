One of the most impactful products I have built from scratch is "Shree Mewa" (GitHub: https://github.com/AgrShubham/ShreeMewa), an end-to-end commercial web application and conversion engine for a luxury artisanal gifting brand.

1. The Core Problem & Product Discovery:
During the initial user research, I noticed that high-ticket gifting (corporate hampers and bespoke wedding boxes valued at ₹10,000–₹50,000) was completely failing on conventional e-commerce checkout flows. Customers dropped off as soon as they encountered rigid address and credit card forms because luxury gifting is fundamentally consultative—buyers wanted specific dry fruit combinations, custom laser engravings, and personalized greeting cards.

2. The Solution & User Journey Redesign:
Applying Design Thinking, I mapped out a new user journey centered on confidence and direct communication:
• Discovery & Customization: Built an atomic, mobile-first UI where users can seamlessly explore luxury collections, configure box materials, and preview custom messages.
• The WhatsApp Concierge Funnel: Instead of forcing an impersonal payment gateway, I engineered an automated concierge checkout pipeline. When a customer finalizes their box, the client-side engine serializes their exact configuration, greeting card text, and quantity into a structured, pre-filled WhatsApp deep-link. This transformed an abandoned cart into an instant, high-trust conversation with the boutique's concierge team.
• On-Demand Corporate Lookbook Engine: Since corporate HRs and procurement managers require offline management sign-offs, I engineered a dedicated print stylesheet (@media print) that formats the digital catalog into a printable, presentation-ready PDF lookbook directly from the browser.

3. My AI-Augmented Prototyping & Workflow ("Vibe Coding"):
I actively leverage modern AI tools to accelerate the product lifecycle:
• Rapid UI Prototyping: Used Claude and ChatGPT to turn wireframe concepts and UX flows into clean, accessible React components with Tailwind CSS.

• Automated Logic & Refactoring: Used AI assistants for unit test scenarios, state machine transitions for the cart customizer, and comprehensive documentation.

4. Tools & Technologies:
• Frontend: React 19, TypeScript 5.8, HTML5, CSS3, Tailwind CSS v4, Vite 6.
• Design & Product: Figma (wireframing, design tokens), Design Thinking, Funnel Optimization.
